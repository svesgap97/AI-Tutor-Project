Informed Consent for Research Participation

Clara is an AI learning assistant used in this course. The instructor is conducting research on how students interact with AI tools to support learning.

  • Your interactions with Clara may be recorded and analyzed for research purposes.
  • The data collected may include your prompts, responses, timestamps, and usage patterns.
  • Your participation is voluntary and will not affect your grade or standing in the course.
  • Research results may be presented in academic publications or conferences.
  • Whenever possible, data will be anonymized or de-identified before analysis.
  • Only authorized members of the research team will have access to the data.
  • You may withdraw your consent and request deletion of your identifiable data at any time, subject to institutional policies.
  • Clara is an AI assistant and may occasionally produce inaccurate information.

By selecting "I Agree", you confirm that you have read and understood this information and consent to participate in this research study.

I am here to support your writing process. I can help you brainstorm ideas, develop characters, build settings, strengthen conflicts, and plan story endings. My job is to help you think, not to write the story for you.

You can take a picture of your notes, worksheets, drafts, story maps, or classroom activities. I can analyze the image and help you understand, expand, or improve your ideas.

If you already have an idea, I can help you make it more interesting. Ask me how to improve a character, add details to a setting, create a stronger conflict, or make your ending more satisfying.

You can share a paragraph or part of your story and ask for feedback. I can help you improve clarity, vocabulary, organization, and description while keeping your ideas and voice as the author.

The more specific your question is, the better I can help. Instead of asking "Help me with my story," try asking "What conflict could my hero face?" or "How can I make this setting feel mysterious?"

When your draft is finished, I can help you revise it. I can point out confusing sections, suggest stronger word choices, identify missing details, and help improve the flow of your story.

Your story belongs to you. I can suggest ideas and ask questions, but the important decisions are yours. The goal is to help you become a more creative and independent writer.
